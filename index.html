01. Philosophy

The Builder's Mindset

I don't just write code; I design systems. My career evolved from automating scripts at Deloitte to architecting multi-tenant SaaS platforms at CloudBolt, and now, running Attiv AI, the studio I founded.

I believe in boring technology for critical paths and cutting-edge innovation for competitive advantage. My current focus is "AI as Infrastructure": treating LLMs not as magic, but as deterministic components in a larger system.

I operate across architecture, production migration, and applied AI: translating complex requirements into reliable systems, owning rollout risk, and reducing incident impact across large-scale environments.

Architecture First

Scalability isn't an afterthought. I design for Day 2 operations, focusing on observability, modularity, and failure domains.

AI-Driven

Pioneering the use of Model Context Protocol (MCP) to give AI agents long-term memory and functional tool access.

Production-Grade

Every solution is built with testing, monitoring, and documentation in mind. No shortcuts, no tech debt without a payoff.
